# BhuNiti design brief

## Design movement
Cinematic civic intelligence: a trustworthy national data platform rendered with quiet confidence, spatial depth and evidence-led clarity.

## Principles
Make complexity legible; pair every signal with provenance; use glow as a focus cue, not decoration; keep motion purposeful; make public-interest software feel premium without feeling flashy.

## Color philosophy
Deep ink/navy backgrounds create focus. Teal indicates live intelligence and trustworthy action. Gold marks policy leverage, evidence strength and human decisions. Muted slate surfaces support long analytical sessions.

## Layout and interaction
Dense but breathable command-center layouts: generous left rail, glass cards, responsive grids, focused charts, layered map controls and clear breadcrumbs. Every interaction should reveal state, provenance or next action.

## Typography and voice
Use a modern sans with strong numeric clarity. Voice is precise, calm and civic: “See the evidence”, “Model the trade-off”, “Move from insight to action”.

## Brand
The BhuNiti wordmark is paired with a contour-grid B mark inspired by land parcels and satellite coordinates. Signature color: evidence teal `#41d7c2`.
